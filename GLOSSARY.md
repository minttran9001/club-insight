# Club insights

Language for what a club host looks at: which sessions went ahead, which players count as active, and which kept bookings were no-shows.

## Language

**Club**:
A venue that hosts sessions for its players.
_Avoid_: Organization, account

**Player**:
A person who belongs to a club and can book a session.
_Avoid_: Member, user

**Session**:
A scheduled block of play at a club, with a start, a duration, and a number of places.
_Avoid_: Game, class, event

**Held session**:
A session that was not cancelled and has already started.
_Avoid_: Completed game, past event

**Booking**:
A player's reservation for one session.
_Avoid_: Registration, ticket

**Kept booking**:
A booking the player did not cancel.
_Avoid_: Active reservation

**Active player**:
A player who attended a held session, or who kept a booking at a held session where attendance was not taken.
_Avoid_: Member, user

**No-show**:
A kept booking on a held session where attendance was taken and the player did not attend.
_Avoid_: Attendance rate, absence

**Week**:
Monday 00:00 through the next Monday 00:00 in the club's time zone.
_Avoid_: Calendar week starting Sunday

**Comparison period**:
The same number of club-local days immediately before the chosen range.
_Avoid_: Previous month, last period
